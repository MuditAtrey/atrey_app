01. Peer-Reviewed Aerodynamic Research

Surrogate-Assisted MDO of Tailless Blended-Wing-Body UAVs

Paper Title: Physics-Regularized Geometric Manifolds for Surrogate-Assisted MDO of Tailless Blended-Wing-Body UAVs
Author: Mudit Atrey (First & Sole Author)
Status: Under external peer review at Aerospace Science and Technology (Elsevier, Impact Factor: 6.4, Q1 Quartile). Manuscript ID: AESCTE-D-26-06017.
Preprint Attribution: Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22893593 • • Download Manuscript (PDF)

Research Formulation & Anti-Exploitation Kernel

Low-order potential flow solvers such as the vortex lattice method (VLM) are widely utilized in conceptual aircraft multidisciplinary design optimization (MDO) due to their low computational cost. However, unconstrained numerical optimizers frequently exploit potential flow assumptions, converging on unphysical airframes with severe camber reflex, unmanufacturable zero-thickness trailing edges, and localized suction singularities that stall in viscous flow.

To prevent solver exploitation, this research introduces a physics-regularized geometric manifold kernel governing spanwise chord, twist, dihedral, and sectional thickness distributions across 5 spanwise stations via C1-continuous Hermite splines. Active learning with a 5-MLP neural surrogate ensemble reduces expensive flow evaluations by >80% over 50,000+ candidate evaluations while enforcing static longitudinal trim (Cm_alpha < 0, static margin ≥ 5% MAC) and dynamic mass-centroid packaging.

3D RANS CFD Cross-Validation & Viscous Drag Decomposition

The optimum design candidate was extracted from the Pareto front and cross-validated against 3D Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) simulations conducted in SimScale and validated against SU2 using the Spalart-Allmaras turbulence model.

  • Viscous Drag Rise: 3D RANS isolated a 124% increase in parasite drag (CD0 = 0.0242) relative to low-fidelity polar drag build-up (CD0 = 0.01077 from Raymer form-factor + XFOIL profile strips). The increase is localized to winglet-root junction pressure concentrations and turbulent wetted area accumulation (0.84 m²).
  • Watertight B-Rep Solid Kernel: Developed an automated OpenCASCADE Python pipeline generating STEP and STL solids with zero micro-gaps and zero-thickness edges, ensuring watertight manifold geometry for automated CFD volume meshing.
  • Surrogate Efficiency: Active-learning surrogate ensemble achieved R² > 0.96 across aerodynamic force coefficients while reducing full aerodynamic solver calls by over 80%.
02. Autonomous Defense Systems & Ballistics

155mm Precision Guided Artillery Trajectory Guidance & Sensor Fusion

Context: Smart India Hackathon 2026 (Ministry of Defence)
Role: Team Leader & Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC) Engineer, Team Narach Dynamics
Objective: Transform conventional unguided 155mm artillery shells into precision strike munitions achieving Circular Error Probable (CEP) ≤ 30m at domestic unit manufacturing cost.

7-DOF Ballistic Dynamics & Mechanical Roll Despin

Standard 155mm artillery projectiles leave the howitzer barrel with a muzzle spin rate of approximately 300 revolutions per second (18,000 RPM) to maintain gyroscopic stability. Steering canards mounted directly on the spinning projectile cannot generate coherent lateral lift vectors without severe actuator bandwidth penalties.

The guidance kit implements a 7-DOF dynamic formulation: 6-DOF rigid projectile flight dynamics coupled with a 1-DOF roll-isolated mechanical steering collar. The forward collar is supported on dual angular contact bearings and damped via passive permanent magnet eddy-current brakes, reducing collar spin from 300 rps to < 5 rps within 1.2 seconds of muzzle exit.

GNC Law & Electronic Warfare Resilience

  • Guidance Law: Augmented Proportional Navigation (APN) commanding two independent pairs of titanium cruciform canards during the terminal descent phase. Demonstrated a 2.34m CEP in simulation over a 20km ballistic trajectory arc under wind gusts and sensor noise injection.
  • State Estimation: Formulated a 16-state Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) fusing 500Hz tactical IMU measurements with GNSS pseudoranges. The filter estimates position (3), velocity (3), attitude quaternion (4), accelerometer bias (3), and gyroscope bias (3).
  • Electronic Warfare Resilience: Evaluated under adversarial GNSS denial / jamming events lasting 25 seconds. The 16-state EKF maintains ballistic dead-reckoning trajectory propagation, limiting dispersion growth to well within operational accuracy thresholds.
  • Mechanical Integration: Designed in Onshape CAD adhering to the NATO standard 2-inch-12UNS fuze thread standard, featuring titanium cruciform canards, dual angular contact bearing raceways, and hermetic slip-rings.
