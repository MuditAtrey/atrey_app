Tailless BWB UAV: Surrogate-Assisted MDO & 3D RANS CFD

Elsevier AST • IF 6.4 • Q1 Quartile

Physics-Regularized Geometric Manifolds for Tailless Blended Wing Body UAVs

First-author manuscript under external peer review at Aerospace Science and Technology (Manuscript ID: AESCTE-D-26-06017; Zenodo Preprint DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22893593). Formulates an active-learning surrogate pipeline that eliminates numerical solver exploitation in autonomous flying wings.

  • Geometric Manifold: C1-continuous Hermite parametric kernel with active-learning 5-MLP surrogate ensemble and dynamic mass-centroid balancing.
  • Efficiency: Slashed solver evaluations by >80% across 50,000+ VLM iterations compared to Latin Hypercube Sampling.
  • 3D RANS Cross-Validation: SimScale incompressible RANS validated against SU2 (Spalart-Allmaras), isolating winglet root pressure concentrations and 124% parasite drag rise (CD0 = 0.0242) over low-fidelity strip polars.
  • Watertight B-Rep Solid Kernel: Custom OpenCASCADE pipeline eliminating micro-gaps and zero-thickness edges for automated CFD meshing.

Project Claymore: 155mm Precision Guided Artillery Fuze Kit

SIH26098 • Team Narach Dynamics Lead

Closed-Loop 7-DOF Trajectory Guidance & 16-State Sensor Fusion

Engineered an indigenous guidance kit transforming unguided 155mm artillery shells into precision strike munitions, achieving ≤ 30m CEP specification at a fraction of the cost of imported M1156 PGK units.

  • 7-DOF Flight Dynamics: Modeled coupled rigid-body aerodynamics with roll-isolated despin collar (7th DOF), cutting spin from 300 rps to < 5 rps.
  • Guidance Law: Augmented Proportional Navigation (APN) steering titanium canards via closed-loop control, reaching simulated 2.34m CEP over 20km+ ballistic trajectories.
  • Electronic Warfare Resilience: 16-state Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) maintaining trajectory estimation during 25-second GNSS denial / jamming events with 500Hz IMU dead-reckoning.
  • CAD & Mechanism: Engineered in Onshape CAD with dual angular contact bearings, hermetic slip-rings, and NATO 2-inch-12UNS fuze thread standard.
